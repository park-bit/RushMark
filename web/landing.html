Inside the studio

Before you start

What does Rushmark cost?

Rushmark is free and open source. Run it on your computer or server. If you connect a paid AI provider, that provider bills you directly.

Which sources can I clip?

Use YouTube, Twitch, Kick, or a video file you have permission to edit. Streams, interviews, and podcasts with clear speech work well.

Can I edit clips before posting?

Yes. Review the preview, trim the start and end, change the captions or layout, and render the clip again before sharing it.
